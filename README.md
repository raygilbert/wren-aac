# Wren AAC

Wren is a free, offline, no-ads AAC app for young children with complex
communication needs and the people who support them.

Wren helps children express themselves, participate, stay safe, and connect
with others through simple, dependable augmentative and alternative
communication.

Tap a symbol, hear it spoken.

No account or internet connection is required after installation.

Open the app: https://raygilbert.github.io/wren-aac/

[How To Install Wren On Your Tablet](#install-wren)

## Why Wren

Communication is foundational to dignity, autonomy, safety, and connection.
Every child deserves a dependable way to express what they think, need, feel,
and want to say.

Wren was inspired by the experience of RBTs supporting families as they
navigate new diagnoses and seek practical communication tools for young
children.

Many AAC tools are expensive, require subscriptions, depend on an internet
connection, or are built around advertising and data collection. Wren is
designed to remove those barriers.

## Our mission

Wren helps make dependable AAC available to every young child who needs
another way to communicate, regardless of a family’s ability to pay or access
to reliable internet.

## Our commitments

Wren is built to last as a public good, not as a product to monetize.

- Free to use
- No advertisements
- No tracking or analytics
- No account required
- Usable offline after installation
- Designed to remain useful for years
- Supports the Open Board Format, making AAC boards easier to move and share

These commitments are intended to make Wren reliable for children, families,
caregivers, educators, and therapists.

## How it works

Wren uses a simple symbol-based interface. A child taps a symbol, and Wren
speaks the associated word or phrase aloud.

The interface is designed to be straightforward for children to use and
practical for caregivers to configure and support.

## A communication tool, not a replacement for people

AAC should support a child’s autonomy and connection with the people around
them. Wren is a tool to help children communicate—not a measure of their
intelligence, ability, or potential.

Children communicate in many different ways. Wren is intended to complement
speech, gestures, signs, facial expressions, writing, and other forms of
communication.

## Open source and shared standards

Wren supports the Open Board Format so that communication boards can be
easier to create, adapt, move, and share across compatible tools.

The goal is to make AAC more accessible and less dependent on a single
application or provider.

## Install Wren

Wren is a Progressive Web App (PWA) — install it straight from your
browser, no app store needed.

**iOS Safari**
1. Open the app link above in Safari.
2. Tap the Share icon.
3. Tap **Add to Home Screen**.

**Android Chrome**
1. Open the app link above in Chrome.
2. Tap **Install app** (or **Add to Home Screen**) from the menu, or tap
   the install banner if Chrome shows one.

**Desktop Chrome / Edge**
1. Open the app link above in Chrome or Edge.
2. Click the install icon in the address bar (usually on the right side).
3. Click **Install**.
